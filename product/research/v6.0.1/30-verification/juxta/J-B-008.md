## Notes on verdict accuracy

- **security/escape lens** returned `survives` with **zero** required changes; nothing to track. Verified: `fetchpriority` rides the same `escapeAttr` schema-key choke point (`src/render/serialize.ts` `buildAttrs` loop) as `src`/`rel`; open unions are compile-time-only and do not loosen runtime escaping; `setType` touches only the attribute, not the script-content sink. No latent escape defect.
- **No killer objection** in any of the three lenses (`killer_objection: null` in all). All three verdicts are `survives` / `survives-with-changes` — the RFC is sound; the changes are curation + completeness, not blockers.
- All RFC code citations were spot-checked and are accurate: `MetaTag.name`/`charset` (`document.ts:62/64`), `LinkTag.rel`/`type`/`as` (`document.ts:102/104/109`), `BaseTag.target` (`document.ts:188`), `ImgTag` (`media.ts:13`), `IframeTag` (`embedded.ts:6`), `ScriptTag.type` (`document.ts:217`). The "only route is `.addAttribute`" and "no `fetchpriority` anywhere" claims hold (grep clean).
