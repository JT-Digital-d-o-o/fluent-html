# Juxtaposition: RFC-A-006 vs. Wave-3 verdicts vs. shipped code

The RFC bundles three fixes (F-A-120 hx-preload escape, F-A-122 setDataAttrs key
validation, F-A-900 script double-escaped openers). As shipped:

- **F-A-120** landed as proposed (`serialize.ts:192-196` wraps the preload string in `escapeAttr`).
- **F-A-122** landed as proposed (`tag.ts:307-314` validates the computed `data-*` key) — but the RFC's
  proto-pollution sub-claim/test were dropped (all three verdicts flagged it false).
- **F-A-900** was **reverted to closer-only** (`serialize.ts:227-242`): the opener-backslash
  neutralization the RFC proposed corrupts benign JS, which the correctness verdict named as its
  killer. The revert is the reconciliation of that killer.

Every `required_change` from all three lenses is satisfied by the shipped (reconciled) state.
