# F-D-160 re-measured through the prior: casing is already absorbed

## Problem
F-D-160 (v6.0.1, parked-major L-144) proposed standardizing compound setters on lowercase tails (`setCrossorigin`, `setHttpequiv`, `setViewbox`) because the mix "defeats IDE autocomplete". The model's JavaScript prior for multi-word attributes is DOM-IDL / React casing (`tabIndex`, `colSpan`, `maxLength`, `crossOrigin`, `httpEquiv`), the opposite of the lowercase-tail majority.

## Evidence (executed)
- Runtime walk of every 8.1.0 setter -> the attribute it writes (156 setters). Classification: 3 intercapped setters on all-lowercase attributes (`setCrossOrigin`/crossorigin, `setFetchPriority`/fetchpriority, `setReferrerPolicy`/referrerpolicy); 19 hyphenated attributes all camelCased (`setHttpEquiv`, `setStrokeWidth`, ...); 11 camelCase SVG attributes kept (`setViewBox`, `setClipPathUnits`, ...); the multi-word rest lowercase-tail (`setTabindex`, `setColspan`, `setMaxlength`, `setAutocomplete`, `setPopovertarget`, `setContenteditable`, ...). So the real inconsistency is 3 names; 2 of F-D-160's 3 examples follow a rule.
- tsc probe (strict, 8.1.0 dist): 28 DOM-IDL guesses on lowercase-tail setters (`setTabIndex`, `setColSpan`, `setMaxLength`, `setAutoComplete`, `setFormAction`, `setContentEditable`, `setPopoverTarget`, `setSrcSet`, `setDateTime`, `setCommandFor`, `setClosedBy`, ...) and 5 lowercase guesses on the intercapped ones (`setCrossorigin`, `setFetchpriority`, `setReferrerpolicy`, `setViewbox`, `setHttpequiv`): 33/33 get `TS2551 Property '<guess>' does not exist on type '<Tag>'. Did you mean '<real>'?`.
- Prior behavior: the pure prior never guessed a compound setter; it wrote `addAttribute("autocomplete", "off")` 5 times across the two runs (3/2) instead ([[F-C-205]] covers that fallback).
- Blast radius (dedup census, fleet / canonical-era): setCrossOrigin 42/23, setFetchPriority 15/8, setReferrerPolicy 2/1 (3 outliers: 59/32); F-D-160 as proposed adds setHttpEquiv 1/0 and setViewBox 266/82 (326/114).

## Why it matters
A case-only miss costs one TS2551 round in either direction, so a codemod-first rename (guardrail 11) of 59 to 326 sites buys consistency, not redirects. The failure mode the prior shows is not guessing the casing but routing through `addAttribute`. Reach 1, impact 1, pain 1.

## Rough idea (not a design)
Keep L-144 parked or close it with this probe as the reason. If a rename ever rides a major, move only the 3 outliers and pick the direction by a prior probe of setter guesses (DOM-IDL casing matches the prior; lowercase-tail matches the attribute); do not rename `setViewBox`/`setHttpEquiv`. No prose change.
