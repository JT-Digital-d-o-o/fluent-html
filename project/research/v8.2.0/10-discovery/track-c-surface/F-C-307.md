# Setter casing is one rule with 3 outliers, and wrong casing already self-heals

## Problem

F-D-160 (L-144) proposed standardizing "lowercase-tail" setter names (`setHttpEquiv`, `setCrossOrigin`, `setViewBox`) and has been parked as a breaking rename since v6.0.1.

A model writing from DOM-IDL memory guesses camelCase for single-token attributes (`setTabIndex`, `setMaxLength`, `setColSpan`). The library spells most of them attribute-verbatim (`setTabindex`, `setMaxlength`, `setColspan`), but spells 3 in IDL camel (`setCrossOrigin`, `setFetchPriority`, `setReferrerPolicy`). That is two conventions for one naming job.

## Evidence (executed)

**Runtime map** (`$C3/probe/setters.mjs`). Every element factory in `dist/` 8.1.0 is instantiated, each `set*` is called with a sample argument, and the attribute name is read back from `render()`:

| Convention | Setters | App canonical sites | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| Attribute verbatim, first letter capitalized | 130 | 5,674 | `setTabindex`→`tabindex`, `setMaxlength`, `setPopovertarget`, `setClipPathUnits`→`clipPathUnits`, `setViewBox`→`viewBox` |
| Hyphenated attribute → camelCase | 19 | 630 | `setHttpEquiv`→`http-equiv`, `setStrokeWidth`, `setTextAnchor` |
| Object / multi-attribute setters | 5 | 790 | `setAria`, `setDataAttrs`, `setHtmx`, `setStyles`, `setClasses` |
| **DOM-IDL camel on a one-token attribute** | **3** | **7** | `setCrossOrigin`→`crossorigin` (3), `setFetchPriority`→`fetchpriority` (3), `setReferrerPolicy`→`referrerpolicy` (1) |
| Unresolved by the probe | 1 | – | `setMicrodata` |

So 149 of 158 follow "spell the attribute; camelCase only across a hyphen". Both names F-D-160 cited as outliers follow it.

**Wrong-casing probe** (`$C3/probe/casing.ts`, `tsc --strict --pretty false`):
- 16 DOM-IDL guesses for verbatim setters;
- 4 verbatim guesses for the IDL and hyphen setters.

```
(3,7):  error TS2551: Property 'setTabIndex' does not exist on type 'Tag'. Did you mean 'setTabindex'?
(6,9):  error TS2551: Property 'setColSpan' does not exist on type 'TdTag'. Did you mean 'setColspan'?
(20,7): error TS2551: Property 'setCrossorigin' does not exist on type 'ImgTag'. Did you mean 'setCrossOrigin'?
(23,8): error TS2551: Property 'setHttpequiv' does not exist on type 'MetaTag'. Did you mean 'setHttpEquiv'?
… 20 of 20 carry a correct 'Did you mean'
```

**Test coverage.** `test/setter-errors.test.ts:52-99` pins unprefixed guesses (`.src`, `.placeholder`, `.autocomplete`, `.viewBox`) but no casing guess.

## Why it matters

- **Scoring:** impact 1 × pain 1 × reach 1 (7 canonical app sites) / effort S(1) = 1.
- **Prior alignment is dented, error quality is not:** every casing miss costs one compile round with the fix in the message.
- **Recommendation:** evidence to close or keep parking L-144 rather than schedule a breaking rename.

## Rough idea (not a design)

- **Park or close L-144** with this measurement.
- **If the 9.0.0 codemod runs anyway**, fold the 3 outliers into the canonical-names rename map. Cost: 7 canonical app sites, 59 fleet. After that, TS2551 still redirects IDL-camel guesses.
- **Either way**, pin the 20 suggestions in `setter-errors.test.ts`. The self-heal depends on TypeScript's spelling-suggestion window, and recon 02 probe 11 shows that window can redirect into a bug (`.colspan` → `colSpan`).
