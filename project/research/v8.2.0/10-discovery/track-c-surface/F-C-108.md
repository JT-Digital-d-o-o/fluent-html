# The lowercase-tail setter rename buys nothing measurable

## Problem
F-D-160 (parked-major since v6.0.1) says compound setters mix intercaps and lowercase tails with "no rule a user can predict" and proposes renaming toward lowercase (`setCrossorigin`, `setHttpequiv`, `setViewbox`). Re-measured on 8.1.0, the surface has three consistent rules, F-D-160 misfiled two of its three examples, and the checker already repairs every casing guess in one hop.

| Rule | Count | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Tail = HTML attribute verbatim | 113 | `setMaxlength` (maxlength), `setTabindex`, `setInputmode`, `setSrcdoc` |
| Hyphenated attribute -> camelCase | 19 | `setHttpEquiv` (http-equiv), `setFillRule`, `setStrokeWidth`, `setFontFamily` |
| SVG camelCase attribute verbatim | 11 | `setViewBox` (viewBox), `setGradientUnits`, `setStdDeviation` |
| **Diverges**: intercaps a single lowercase token | 3 | `setCrossOrigin` 42/23, `setFetchPriority` 15/8, `setReferrerPolicy` 2/1 (fleet / canonical) |

All 3 divergent names are the DOM-IDL spelling (`crossOrigin`, `fetchPriority`, `referrerPolicy`), as are 17 names the verbatim rule lowercases (`maxLength`, `tabIndex`, `colSpan`, `inputMode`, `formMethod`, ...). A model's DOM/React prior therefore guesses the IDL spelling for 20 setters. `setHttpEquiv` is legacy-only (1 fleet / 0 canonical).

## Evidence (executed)
- `node setter-attrs.mjs`: 158 `set*` names; 146 resolve to exactly one attribute by render; 143/146 follow one of the three rules; 8 are multi-attribute/unresolved and 4 are not attribute setters.
- `casing.ts`: the IDL-cased guess for each of the 17 verbatim setters and the lowercase guess for each of the 3 divergent ones, on an owning receiver: 20/20 `TS2551 ... Did you mean '<correct setter>'?` (e.g. `Property 'setMaxLength' does not exist on type 'InputTag'. Did you mean 'setMaxlength'?`, `Property 'setCrossorigin' does not exist on type 'AudioTag'. Did you mean 'setCrossOrigin'?`). The suggestion is the right fix every time; case-insensitive matches are exempt from the spelling window that defeats `.src`/`.value`.
- Rename cost (dedup census): lowercasing the 3 divergent names touches 59 fleet sites (32 canonical); moving the 17 verbatim names to IDL case touches 312 (maxlength 129, minlength 125, tabindex 40, ...).

## Why it matters
impact 1, pain 1 (a casing miss costs one TS2551 round trip that names the fix), reach 2 (20 setters). The rename would spend a codemod over 59 or 312 sites and a breaking entry in the one bundled major for no measured change in error quality; the prior misses it would fix are already absorbed by the checker. Current state hurts prior alignment only by that one round trip.

## Rough idea (not a design)
Close F-D-160 without renaming. Protect the property that makes it harmless: add the 20 casing guesses to `test/setter-errors.test.ts` (type layer) so a future rename of a neighbouring name that breaks the case-insensitive match fails CI. No prose.
