# No HTML/ARIA platform coverage watch

`$D3` = `<scratch>/wave1/D3`

## Problem

The class half of the library has a contract: a Tailwind oracle pinned to 4.3.3 (`vocab-validity`), plus a coverage watch that fails when a new utility root appears until it is covered or listed in `IGNORED_ROOTS` with a reason (`vocab-coverage.test.ts`).

The HTML/ARIA half has none. It consists of:
- 22 closed unions in `html-types.ts` and the closed `AriaAttributeName`.
- 132 element names' schema keys.
- The `Tag` global setters.

All are hand-maintained, and 0 files in `test/`, `scripts/`, `src/` or `package.json` consult platform data.

Closed unions are the house style ("a typo is a compile error"). The flip side is that a closed union also rejects every *valid* platform name added after its audit date. Nothing tells the maintainers when that happens, and nothing tells an agent whether a typed feature works on every engine.

## Evidence (executed)

**Attribute coverage.** `coverage.mjs` diffs BCD 8.1.3 and web-features 3.40.0 against the runtime walk of `dist/` 8.1.0. It covers standard-track, non-deprecated, non-experimental attribute keys on the 132 factory elements, plus the global attributes: 272 keys.

| | Baseline (high/low) | Not Baseline |
|---|---|---|
| Typed | 220 | 13 |
| Untyped | **24** | 15 |

**The 24 untyped Baseline keys.**

18 already sit in the ledger or a recorded decision:
- L-169: `input.alt/formaction/formenctype/formmethod/formtarget/src`
- L-175: `li.value`, `ol.start/type`
- L-269: `template.shadowrootmode/clonable/delegatesfocus`, `part`, `exportparts`, `slot`
- L-271: `img.usemap`
- L-202: global `inputmode`
- v6.2.0 RFC-B-07 skip: `accesskey`

**6 are recorded nowhere:**
- `autocorrect`: Baseline **2026-09-11** (F-D-306)
- `input.webkitdirectory`: Baseline 2025-07-22
- `form.rel`
- `form.name`
- `form.accept-charset`
- `input.size`

**Closed unions reject names that all 3 engines ship: 7** (tsc 5.9.3 probe):
- 4 ARIA names (F-D-303): TS2353 once, TS2561 three times, each with a did-you-mean to a different attribute.
- `.toggle("shadowrootclonable")` (C124 F125 S17.5): TS2345.
- `.toggle("shadowrootdelegatesfocus")` (C123 F123 S16.4): TS2345.
- `.toggle("webkitdirectory")` (C7 F50 S11.1): TS2345.

**Typed but non-Baseline: 13 keys, none of them marked.** The one taught as a replacement for JS is `dialog.closedby` (Safari `preview`, see F-D-301). `hidden="until-found"` (web-features: not Baseline, Safari 26.2 partial) is taught as "Ctrl-F-revealable" at `guidelines/web-development/fluent-html.md:90`.

**Open unions offer dead names: 4.**
- `LinkElementRel` `'prerender'`: BCD deprecated + non-standard.
- `PermissionsPolicyDirective` `'ambient-light-sensor'` and `'magnetometer'`: 0 of 3 engines.
- `'attribution-reporting'`: deprecated + non-standard.

**Drift since 2026-06** (`since.mjs 2026-06-01`), each reached no lib file:
- `autocorrect` became Baseline.
- `popover=hint` reached a second engine (F-D-307).
- `<selectedcontent>` reached a second engine (F-D-308).
- `img sizes=auto` reached all three engines (already accepted by `setSizes(string)`).

## Why it matters

- **reach 2**: 7 rejected valid names + 6 unrecorded Baseline keys + 13 unmarked non-Baseline keys.
- **impact 2** and **pain 2**: this silence is what produced F-D-301, F-D-303 and F-D-306.

Dimensions:
- **evolvability**: the platform moves every 4-6 weeks, and the unions move only when someone audits.
- **verification-loop**: no build step can see the drift. It is the HTML twin of guardrail 10 and of the Tailwind coverage watch.

## Rough idea (not a design)

**CI layer.** A `platform-coverage.test.ts` that loads a pinned BCD + web-features snapshot. Both are devDependencies, so guardrail 1 is untouched. It then:
1. Diffs every closed union member and schema key.
2. Fails on a Baseline name that is neither typed nor in an `IGNORED` list carrying a reason, which is exactly the vocab-coverage shape.
3. Emits the Baseline status per typed name into the generated docs, so `setClosedby` and `setHidden` carry a support note without hand-written prose.

The snapshot bump is the review trigger, the same as the tailwindcss pin.
