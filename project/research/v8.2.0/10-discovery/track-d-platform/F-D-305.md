# Implicit anchor makes the taught anchorName + positionAnchor pair redundant for invoker popovers

`$D3` = `<scratch>/wave1/D3`

## Problem

The guidelines and both CLAUDE.md copies teach three calls and an `Id` coupling to place a popover under its trigger: "reuse one `Id`: `.anchorName(id)` on the trigger, `.positionAnchor(id).positionArea("bottom")` on the popover".

Since 2026-01-13 (Baseline low, web-features 3.40.0), a popover shown by a `popovertarget` or `commandfor` invoker gets that invoker as its **implicit anchor**. `position-area` alone therefore places it, and the other two calls are redundant for the case the teaching illustrates.

The surrounding text is also stale. It says the methods emit classes (`[anchor-name:--user-menu]`, `position-area-bottom`) and instructs agents to safelist "those two classes" in the build. The lib has emitted inline style since the L-055 fix (`tailwind-methods.ts:476-481`: "All three emitters write *inline style*, not a Tailwind class"), so the safelist step targets classes that are never emitted.

## Why it matters

- **reach 2**: 5 repos, plus teaching lines loaded in every guided session.
- **impact 1**: the redundant form still works.
- **pain 2**: the stale safelist instruction sends an agent to edit build config for classes that do not exist.

Dimensions:
- **context-economy**: about 12 lines teach a 3-call pattern where 1 call suffices.
- **decision-closure**: an extra Id coupling to keep consistent.

## Rough idea (not a design)

**Prose deletion** (guideline delta about -12 lines):
- For invoker-owned popovers, teach `.positionArea("bottom")` on the popover only.
- Keep `anchorName`/`positionAnchor` for non-invoker anchors, in one line.
- Delete the class-emission claims and the safelist paragraph.

No lib change. The JSDoc at `tailwind-methods.ts:476-481` is already correct. Optionally, `gl/htmx.md` can point at the lib test that pins the inline-style output.
