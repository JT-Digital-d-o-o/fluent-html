# 61 of 106 canonical .cssProp() sites spell a named Tailwind utility

## Problem
`.cssProp()` is the hatch for "CSS property with no Tailwind utility". In the canonical era, most of its uses spell a property Tailwind 4.3.3 does have a utility for: the vocab lacks the row, or pruned it. Those sites are invisible to the decided family-keyed merger and to conflict detection. They cost more tokens than the typed call, and they teach the next agent that the hatch is the way.

## Evidence (executed)
- `census.mjs` over the 16 canonical-era dedup repos, app-authored files only (recon 01's filter): 106 `.cssProp("prop","value")` literal sites, 0 `eslint-disable` for the raw-class rule.
- `cssprop-map.mjs` compiles every 4.3.3 `getClassList()` class, indexes single-declaration utilities by `property:value` (spacing normalized to rem), and matches each site. 61/106 have an exact named utility:

| property:value | sites | repos | 4.3.3 utility | vocab status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| vertical-align:top/middle | 12 | 5 | align-top/middle | IGNORED backlog `align` |
| scroll-margin-top:* | 11 | 6 | scroll-mt-28/14/6/24/11 | pruned 8.0.0 (scrollM, 0 sites then); hidden by `scroll` prefix |
| flex:3..7 | 11 | 1 (everyframe-composer) | flex-3..flex-7 | watch-covered, untyped |
| border-collapse:separate/collapse | 9 | 8 (7 are template copies) | border-separate/collapse | watch-covered, untyped |
| flex-basis:* | 3 | 2 | basis-8/14/32 | IGNORED backlog `basis` |
| scroll-snap-align:* | 3 | 2 | snap-start/center | pruned 8.0.0 |
| scrollbar-width:none | 3 | 2 | scrollbar-none | IGNORED backlog `scrollbar` (4.3.0 root) |
| transform-origin / transform-box / object-position / background-repeat | 1 each | | origin-center, transform-view, object-top, bg-no-repeat | backlog / untyped |
| word-break:break-all (3), pointer-events:none, overscroll-behavior:contain | 5 | | break-all, pointer-events-none, overscroll-contain | **typed today** |

- The scroll-margin-top sites are independently authored across 6 repos (competify 4, fl-um 2, popri 2, everyframe 1, gzs/stem-50 1, sportoawards 1). The `border-collapse` demand is 3 authored sites (the template's `cohorts.view.ts`, everyframe-composer captions, fluent-html-home-page).

## Why it matters
impact 1 × pain 1 × reach 3 / S = 3. The output renders correctly, so impact is low. The breadth is real: 10 of 16 canonical repos use `.cssProp()` for a named utility. The census is the evidence L-159 and L-223 set as their reopen conditions.

## Rough idea (not a design)
Vocab rows, class-string contract (guardrail 9), evidence-gated per S-23. Re-add scroll-m (6 repos) and add `align` (5 repos). Promote numeric `flex` and `border-collapse`/`separate` on this evidence, and leave `basis`/`snap` on the backlog at 2 repos each. Extend `no-fluent-equivalent-in-setstyle` to `.cssProp()` pairs that have a typed spelling (the 5 typed-reachable sites). Each promotion deletes its backlog line in `vocab-generator/backlog.md`.
