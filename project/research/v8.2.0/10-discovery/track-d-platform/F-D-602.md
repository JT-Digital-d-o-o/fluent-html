# The lib's type contract is verified on TypeScript 5.9.3 only

`$R` = `<scratch>/wave1/D-reseed`

## Problem

fluent-html's strongest enforcement layer is the type checker: closed unions, brands, did-you-mean diagnostics, and two suites that pin diagnostic text (`test/setter-errors.test.ts`, `test/brand-errors.test.ts`). All of it is verified on TypeScript 5.9.3 (`package.json:135`, `"typescript": "^5.0.0"`, a range that cannot reach 6 or 7). There is no `typescript` peer range. The consumers compile on other majors:

- 15 of 15 canonical apps and the template's full-stack scaffold declare `^6.0.3` and install 6.0.3.
- npm `latest` is 7.0.2 (2026-07-08), the native compiler; `next` is 7.1.0-dev.

Three breaks follow, none visible to the lib's CI:

1. **The lib's own `tsconfig.json` does not compile on 6.0.3 or 7.0.2.** TS 6 stopped auto-including `@types/*`, and the config sets no `types`.
2. **The diagnostic pins cannot load on 7.0.2.** They call `ts.createProgram`; `typescript@7.0.2` exports only `version` and `versionMajorMinor`. Their text also moves: 2 `brand-errors` regexes expect `'ResolvedRoute ...'` where TS 7 prints `'ExternalHref | ResolvedRoute'`.
3. **typescript-eslint refuses TS 7.0.** An app that moves to 7 loses all 32 fluent-html lint rules at once.

On the other side, TS 7 shortens the two worst probed diagnostics with no lib change, and nothing in the lib measures or pins that.

## Evidence (executed)

Compilers installed in `$R/ts/` (6.0.3, 7.0.2); the lib's own 5.9.3.

| Command | 5.9.3 | 6.0.3 | 7.0.2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| `tsc --noEmit -p tsconfig.json` (src + test + bench + scripts) | exit 0 (2.82 s) | 214 errors (2.65 s) | 214 errors (0.52 s) |
| same, `--types node` | n/a | 0 | 0 |
| `-p test/types/color-optout` | 0 | 5 errors; 0 with `--types node` | 5; 0 |
| `-p src/behaviors/client` | 0 | 2; 0 | 2; 0 |

The 214 are TS2591 x132 and TS2584 x59 (no `process`, `console`, `node:stream`) plus 23 cascades, and all vanish with `--types node`. The type surface itself is compatible; the build config is not.

Pinned probes (CLI with the lib's flags, `--ignoreConfig` on 6 and 7):
- `test/types/setter-probe/probe.ts`: 12 diagnostics, byte-identical on 5.9.3, 6.0.3 and 7.0.2.
- `test/types/brand-probe/probe.ts`: 5 diagnostics, identical on 5.9.3 and 6.0.3. On 7.0.2 all 5 differ: union order becomes `'ExternalHref | ResolvedRoute'`, and the `.setHtmx` TS2769 drops both overload listings for `The last overload gave the following error.` (column 7 to 15). `brand-errors.test.ts:59` and `:64` (`/type 'ResolvedRoute/`) would fail on that text.
- `typescript@7.0.2` main export: `version`, `versionMajorMinor`; no `createProgram`.

Recon-02 phase-3 fixtures (33 files, in a scaffold copy at `$R/app`), `tsc -p` on 6.0.3 vs 7.0.2. 18 files error on both; 14 are identical and 4 differ:

| Probe | 6.0.3 | 7.0.2 |
|---|---|---|
| p02 missing `Match` case | TS2769, 7 lines / 1354 chars, `Property 'success' is missing` at char 1027 | TS2769, 4 lines / 747 chars, at char 420 (Overload-1 detour gone) |
| p20 non-exhaustive `defineController` | TS2345, 2 lines / 1407 chars, key at char 712 | TS2741 `Property 'invite' is missing...`, 1 line / 704 chars, key at char 9 |
| p10 raw `setHref` | `'ResolvedRoute \| ExternalHref \| undefined'` | `'ExternalHref \| ResolvedRoute \| undefined'` |
| p29 fragment stance | em dash printed as `—` | printed as the character |

Whole-scaffold typecheck: 2.16 s on 6.0.3, 0.49 s on 7.0.2.

Lint on TS 7 (`$R/app7`: the scaffold with `node_modules/typescript` pointing at 7.0.2, `--preserve-symlinks`): `eslint src/app/home` exits 2 with `typescript-eslint does not support TS 7.0.` (ESLint 10.11.0). The same files on 6.0.3: 0 messages.

## Why it matters

impact 2 x pain 2 x reach 3 (every consumer compiles on a different major than the one the lib verifies).

- **evolvability**: the 6 to 7 move is unplanned, and the build already fails on 6.
- **verification-loop**: the error-text pins, which B-track RFCs will extend, cannot run on the current major.
- **error-quality**: TS 7 alone moves the two B-track headline probes (Match fix from char 1027 to 420, controller key from 712 to 9), so any error-text RFC measured on 6.0.3 needs a TS 7 column.

## Rough idea (not a design)

ci layer, no public change (8.1.x). Pin the dev compiler to the fleet's (6.0.3) and add `"types": ["node"]` to the lib tsconfig. Run the diagnostic pins through `tsc --pretty false` output instead of `ts.createProgram`, with union-order-insensitive matching. Add a TS 7 typecheck job over `test/types` and the probes. Record the template's move to 7 as blocked on typescript-eslint, which exits on 7.0.
