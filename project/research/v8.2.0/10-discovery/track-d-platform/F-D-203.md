# Coverage watch counts roots as covered that no typed argument can emit

## Problem
The coverage watch exists so that "a Tailwind bump that introduces new utility families fails here until each new root is added to the vocab or ignored on purpose" (`test/vocab-coverage.test.ts` header). A root counts as covered when it starts with any prefix a vocab row owns (`border`, `scroll`, `inset`, `font`, `bg`, `scale`, `rotate`, `justify`, `items`, `min-w`, ...). So a new family under an existing prefix, or a pruned family under a kept prefix, is invisible. Value-level additions (new palette families, new scale keys) have no check at all.

## Evidence (executed)
- Registries dumped with `__unstable__loadDesignSystem` for 7 Tailwind versions (roots, variants, `getClassList()`): 4.0.17 has 252/798/77/12,828; 4.3.3 has 315/890/88/23,286. 4.3.0 through 4.3.3 are identical (0 roots, 0 variants, 0 classes differ).
- Class-level reachability. The typed enumeration (F-D-201 harness: 112,036 calls) gives 11,768 compiling classes. Joined with every candidate root of every 4.3.3 class (`parseCandidate`, all parses):
  - 1,073 roots: 673 covered by prefix, 48 covered exactly, 352 ignored.
  - 214 watch-covered roots have at least one `getClassList()` class and 0 typed-reachable classes. The largest: `border-s`, `border-e`, `border-bs`, `border-be` (296 classes each), `ring-offset` (296), `border-spacing` (105, plus x/y 35 each), 15 `scroll-m*`/`scroll-p{s,e,bs,be}` roots (35 each), `inset-bs`/`inset-be` (63 each), `font-stretch` (19), `scale-x/y/z` (11 each), `rotate-x/y/z` (9 each), `perspective-origin` (9), plus 150+ static roots such as `bg-cover`, `bg-no-repeat`, `content-center`, `justify-items-*`, `justify-self-*`, `text-start`, `text-ellipsis`, `h-lh`, `items-center-safe`, `space-x-reverse`, `border-collapse`, `table-caption`.
  - Classes: 13,060 non-negative classes sit under watch-covered roots. 4,584 of them are unreachable after removing 26 enumeration fill gaps. 1,320 of the 4,584 are the palette families `mauve`, `mist`, `olive`, `taupe`, added in 4.2.0 and absent from `TailwindColorName` (template.txt:64). That gap is inert for 14/15 canonical apps, which set `defaultPalette: false`.
- Replay of a bump through the current watch (`replay.mjs`, current vocab, old vs new registry). 4.1.18 to 4.3.3: 114 new roots; the watch flags 87 and 27 pass silently. Those 27 carry 984 classes, 86 of them typed-reachable (the `inset-s`/`inset-e` family). 19 silent roots have no typed spelling: `border-bs`, `border-be`, `inset-bs`/`inset-be` (+auto/full/px), `scroll-mbs`/`mbe`/`pbs`/`pbe` (+px), `font-features`. Another 2,201 new classes landed under pre-existing roots, and 1,365 of them are watch-covered and typed-unreachable. For 4.2.4 to 4.3.3 the watch flags 10/10 (brand-new prefixes). For 4.0.17 to 4.3.3: 229 new roots, 174 flagged, 55 silent.
- Two 8.0.0 prunes never reached IGNORED_ROOTS. L-218 (scaleX/rotateX/perspectiveOrigin) and L-223 (scrollM) are hidden by the kept `scale`/`rotate`/`perspective`/`scroll` prefixes. IGNORED_ROOTS lists `skew` and `backface` from the same prune.
- Fleet reach (`cssprop-map.mjs`). In the 16 canonical-era repos (app-authored), 106 `.cssProp()` sites exist and 61 spell an exact named 4.3.3 utility. 34 of those 61 sit under roots the watch reports as covered: `border-separate`/`border-collapse` 9, `flex-<n>` 11, `scroll-mt-*` 11, `transform-view`, `bg-no-repeat`, `object-top` 1 each. The watch said these were handled, and each author reached for the hatch instead.
- The watch has never run across a bump. The pin has been 4.3.3 since the oracle's first commit f840c9f (2026-07-31), and 4.3.3 is still npm latest (published 2026-07-16).

## Why it matters
impact 2 × pain 2 × reach 2 / M = 4. This is the evolvability guard for Tailwind drift, and it reports green over 214 roots and 4,584 classes the typed surface cannot express. It would have passed 27 of the 114 roots added since 4.1.18 without a signal. Its first real test comes at the next bump.

## Rough idea (not a design)
CI layer, test-only. Replace prefix coverage with class-level coverage: a root is covered iff some typed argument emits one of its `getClassList()` classes. The checker-driven enumeration used here runs in about 1 minute over the dist types. Every other root goes into IGNORED_ROOTS with a reason, which also makes the two missing 8.0.0 prune records explicit. Add a value-level diff for theme namespaces (`--color-*` families, spacing/radius/shadow keys) against the typed unions. Deletes no prose; lets `vocab-coverage.ts:12-14` drop its "intentionally over-covers" caveat.
