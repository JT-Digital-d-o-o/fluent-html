# The RootedView alias is the expensive part of the 8.1.0 root brand

## Problem
The 8.1.0 root brand (`setId(ids.x)` -> `this & Rooted<"x">`, checked against `RootedView<"x">` by `renderFragment` and the template's `fragment()` helper) is the single largest fluent-html contribution to typecheck cost in the fleet's largest 8.1 app. `RootedView<N>` is an aliased intersection whose `View` half is a union, so the target distributes into `(Rooted<"x"> & string) | (Rooted<"x"> & Tag) | (Rooted<"x"> & View[])` (visible verbatim in the elaboration), and relating `Tag & Rooted<"x">` to that shape walks every Tag member under a fresh `this`. The trace shows it: 42,453 of the app's types are method signatures declared in `tailwind-methods.d.ts`, each method instantiated for 231-235 distinct `this` types, of which 201/235 (for `flex`) are Rooted intersections such as `Rooted<"plan-desk"> & Tag & Rooted<...>`.

## Evidence (executed)
Synthetic, 50 components each (tsc 6.0.3, `--extendedDiagnostics`):

| Variant | types | instantiations | check |
|---|---|---|---|
| c: `function R(): RootedView<"r"> { return Div(...).setId(ids.r) }` | 57,088 | 679,040 | 0.77-0.92 s |
| g: same, annotated inline `Tag & Rooted<"r">` | 682 | 2,034 | 0.07 s |
| a/b/d/e: setId alone, chained after setId, passed to `View`, `: View` | 581-1,480 | 1,933-2,234 | 0.04-0.06 s |
| c with `RootedView = Tag & Rooted<N>` (still an aliased intersection) | 30,278 | 340,680 | 0.45 s |
| c with `RootedView = [N] extends [string] ? Tag & Rooted<N> : never` | 833 | 2,489 | 0.07 s |
| c with brand off (`Rooted<N> = unknown`) | 462 | 1,773 | 0.04 s |

Linear in annotated components: K = 25 / 50 / 100 -> 30,192 / 56,882 / 110,267 types and 339,686 / 678,986 / 1,357,586 instantiations (about 1,100 types and 13,570 instantiations per check).

Whole app, everyframe-composer (555 .ts files, 196,439 lines; overlay copy reproduces the in-place baseline exactly: 338,749 types, 1,150,634 instantiations):

| lib variant | types | instantiations | errors |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8.1.0 as shipped | 338,749 | 1,150,634 | 0 |
| brand off (upper bound of the brand's cost) | 283,140 (-16.4%) | 920,959 (-20.0%) | 0 |
| `RootedView = [N] extends [string] ? Tag & Rooted<N> : never` | 298,703 (-11.8%) | 984,388 (-14.4%) | 0 |

Check time, 4 interleaved runs under shared load: 5.52/5.59/5.96/6.06 s vs 5.33/5.34/5.60/5.66 s (mean 5.78 -> 5.49 s). Scaffolded template (auth only, 1 app-authored fragment site): 90,504 -> 88,604 types. Reach: `renderFragment` has 143 sites in 10 of 14 8.1 apps (typed census).

Semantics: with the conditional encoding in `src/ids.ts`, the lib's `npx tsc --noEmit -p tsconfig.json` (includes every RootedView/Partial/`: View` assertion in `type-surface.test-d.ts`) exits 0; a union id (`setId(c ? ids.a : ids.b)` into `RootedView<"a" | "b">`) still compiles; the 4 error probes still fail, with shorter text: wrong root 6 -> 4 lines, missing setId 4 -> 2 lines (the distributed `... & string | ... & View[]` elaboration disappears).

## Why it matters
impact 2 x pain 1 x reach 2 / S = 4. Verification loop: every agent runs tsc (recon 02: 4/4 in-repo agents reached for it); the brand's cost grows about 15 ms per annotated fragment component, and `RootedView` is the annotation the JSDoc tells authors to reach for (`src/ids.ts:40-67`). Evolvability: the next brand built the same way multiplies the same walk.

## Rough idea (not a design)
Type layer: re-encode `RootedView<N>` so the target is not a distributed aliased union (the measured candidate: `[N] extends [string] ? Tag & Rooted<N> : never`), and add a type-cost budget test (types/instantiations of a fixed fixture via `--extendedDiagnostics`) so a future contract change reports its cost. No prose to delete.
