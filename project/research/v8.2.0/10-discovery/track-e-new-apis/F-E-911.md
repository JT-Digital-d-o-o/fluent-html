# Form<T> stamps the constraints its body schema enforces

($EG = <scratch>/track-e/E-gaps)

## Peer capability

- **Django forms**: a `CharField(max_length=200)` renders `maxlength="200"`, and a required field renders `required`, from the one definition the server validates with.
- **Phoenix**: `Phoenix.HTML.Form.input_validations/2` derives `required`, `maxlength` and `minlength` from the changeset's validations.
- **fluent-html + template**: the route's TypeBox body is the server's validator and `Form<T>` binds every control by the same field names, but nothing carries the schema's limits to the controls, so each view restates them or forgets.

## Problem

home-page's contact form validates its body with `maxLength` caps (contact.schema.ts:6-11: name 200, email 320, company 200, message 5000). Its controls go through a field helper (`f.input(name, type)`, contact.view.ts:102; `f.textarea("message")`, :114) and emit no `maxlength`. A visitor who pastes 5,001 characters is not stopped by the browser. Fastify's schema check fails before the handler, the template's error handler maps any validation failure to a 400 ErrorPage (core/server/server.ts:356) aimed at `#main-content`, and htmx 4 swaps it (`noSwap: [204, 304]` in the served 4.0.0 bundle). The form and everything typed are gone. The same visitor with a 5,000-character message and a bad email gets the app's own 422, which keeps the form and the values. The schema and the control disagree, and the user's input pays for it.

The template ships the same gap: `username: Type.Optional(Type.String({ maxLength: 30 }))` (account.schema.ts:12) is bound as `Label("Username", f.input("username", "text"))` (account.page.view.ts:90), with the limit only in the prose under the field ("3 to 30 characters"); everyframe and workshop-toni carry the copy.

## Evidence (executed)

1. **Runtime** (`node $EG/constraints/hp/inject.mjs`: home-page dist booted through its own `buildServer()` in a scratch cwd, `DATABASE_URL` pointed at a dead port so nothing is written):
   - `GET /contact` 200, 0 `maxlength` attributes in the form.
   - `POST /contact`, HX-Request, 5,001-char message: **400**, body carries "Please check your input", no `<form>`, the typed name is absent, 1 `#main-content`.
   - `POST /contact`, 5,000-char message, email `not-an-email`: **422**, `<form>` present, typed name kept.
2. **Static** (`node $EG/constraints/scan2.mjs`: each `Form<X>` resolved through `type X = Static<typeof Body>` to its `Type.Object` literal; each direct `f.input/textarea("field")` chain read for the matching setter):

   | schema key | bound directly | stamped by hand | omitted |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | maxLength | 74 | 35 (13 repos), 0 differ from the schema | 39 (11 repos) |
   | minLength | 187 | 184 (`toggle("required")` / `setMinlength`) | 3 |
   | minimum | 10 | 4 | 6 (5 repos) |
   | maximum | 5 | 4 | 1 |

   Totals: 317 `Form<T>` forms, 368 constrained fields, 248 bound directly, 120 bound through helpers the scan cannot resolve (home-page contact is one of them, and renders 0 `maxlength`). The critic's first pass reported 4 differing values; resolved per form, 0 differ (the 4 were field-name collisions across forms). 0 canonical repos use `attachValidation`, so no app intercepts the 400.
3. **Prototype** (`$EG/constraints/form-schema.diff`, 36 lines in a scratch copy of the lib): `FormState<T>` gains `schema?: FormSchema<T>`; `input`/`textarea` stamp before returning. `$EG/constraints/probe/probe.ts` compiles with TypeBox objects passed as they are (`Form<ContactReq>({ schema: ContactBody }, ...)`, no cast), a mistyped schema is a compile error (`@ts-expect-error` holds), and the render stamps `maxlength="5000"` on the message, `maxlength="80" required` on the profile name, `min="1" max="99"` on a number input; an explicit `.setMaxlength(80)` after binding wins. The scratch lib's forms, form-for, class-vocab and vocab suites pass 1007/1007.

## Why it matters

impact 3: a valid user action (pasting a long message) destroys the user's input, and the developer sees only a logged validation error. pain 2. reach 3: omissions in 11 repos, hand restatements in 15. Hurts silent-failure, invariant-safety (two sources of truth for one limit, 35 copies that can drift) and context-economy (227 restatements).

## Rough idea (not a design)

Runtime by construction: the binding stamps what the schema enforces, so the omission cannot be written; the type layer keys `FormSchema<T>` by `T`, so the schema of another body fails to compile. Open points: `pattern` (JSON Schema is unanchored, HTML `pattern` is anchored: translate or skip; the prototype skips), `required` only when the field is in `required` and `minLength >= 1` (an Optional field must stay submittable empty), and allocation only when a schema is passed (render hot path, guardrail 2). No guideline prose covers this today; the template's auth and account views drop their restated literals.
