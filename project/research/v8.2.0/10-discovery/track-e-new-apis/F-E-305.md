# Bounded job progress is polled; one-shot streaming answers it in one request

## Problem

Canonical apps watch bounded jobs (renders, evaluations, redaction runs, a payment status) with `.when(!terminal, t => t.poll(route, every))`. There are 14 such sites in 7 canonical repos. Each tick is a request, a full fragment render, and a draw on `pollRateLimit`.

htmx 4.0.0's `hx-sse` extension takes over any normal `hx-post`/`hx-get` response whose content type is `text/event-stream`. Without `hx-sse:connect` it is one-shot (`reconnect: false`, `releaseOn: 'end'`), and it swaps every unnamed frame into the request's target with the request's swap. That is exactly the "one-shot streamed response" the htmx4 capability scan called the usable real-time shape.

L-263 gates it on a prod spike. The client half is now executable on the GA bundle, and two of the scan's three de-risking mechanics, measured on beta6, no longer hold on 4.0.0.

## Evidence (executed)

- **Census** (`g.mjs '\.poll\('`, canonical app code):
  - There are 31 `.poll` sites in 9 repos.
  - 14 are bounded, wrapped in a terminal or in-flight `.when` or attached to an in-progress panel, across 7 repos: everyframe-composer 8, fl-um, popri, projects-template, studio, website-sales-funnel and workshop-toni 1 each.
  - The 11 dashboard-widget polls in website-sales-funnel-automation-system are continuous and out of scope.
- **Runtime oracle** (`p7.mjs`, `out-p7.json`). A real node http server streams 3 frames 600 ms apart, each a `<p>` plus a `<template hx type="partial">`. A button `hx-post="/job" hx-indicator="#ind"` starts it, with each bundle's own `hx-sse.js` loaded:
  - On both bundles there is exactly 1 `POST /job` (`accept: text/html, text/event-stream`). The panel reads `step 1/3`, `step 2/3`, `step 3/3` at 450, 1050 and 1650 ms, the partial region reads `1`, `2`, `3`, and nothing reconnects in the 2.5 s after the stream ends.
  - beta6: `#ind` is not busy during the stream, and a second request queued on the source (`hx-sync="#go:queue"`) runs at +1669 ms, mid-stream.
  - 4.0.0: `#ind` carries `htmx-request` until the stream ends, and the queued request waits until +2140 ms, after the end. In htmx.js 4.0.0, `finally` awaits `ctx.extensionPromise` before `hideIndicators` and `requestQueue.continue()`. The scan's "indicators clear at stream start" and "the stream does not hold the element's request queue" are therefore false for the served bundle.
- **Fluent frames** (`p7b.mjs`). Frames built as `render([Div(P(...), P(...)).setId("job-panel"), Partial(ids.count, ...)])` and split into `data:` lines stream progressively into `#job-panel` with `outerMorph` on both bundles (3/3 frames, partial region 1, 2, 3).
- **Sketch** (`tpl/src/core/htmx/stream.ts`, tsc exit 0): `Button("Render").stream(ids.jobPanel, routes.run())` emits `hx-post="/jobs/run" hx-target="#job-panel" hx-swap="outerMorph" hx-indicator="#job-panel"`. A mismatched region is a consumed `@ts-expect-error`.
- **Caveat found while probing:** hx-sse stops after the first frame when a swap disconnects the source element (`while (element.isConnected)`). The verb must therefore not use `outerHTML` on the source itself, which is `.poll`'s swap.

## Why it matters

impact 3 x pain 2 x reach 2 / L = 4.

- Request count is the host's scarce resource (the scan puts the account at 84% of its NPROC budget). A bounded job watched at 2 s for 60 s is 30 requests today and 1 streamed request with this shape.
- **evolvability:** the GA bundle changed behavior the gate assumed. The indicator change and the queue change make a verb that owns its busy state simpler, but they mean a second gesture on the same element waits for the job.

## Rough idea (not a design)

Keep the gate: the LSAPI connection-lifetime spike L-263 asks for is still the deliverable. When it clears, add a template `.stream(target, route)` verb, an `sseFrame(view)` reply helper, and vendored `hx-sse.js`. Leave `.poll` for continuous widgets. Nothing changes in the lib. Record the two reversed mechanics in the scan.
