# Template fragments for the 422 half: one answer shape instead of a branch

($E9 = <scratch>/track-e/E9)

## Peer capability

- **templ** `@templ.Fragment("name")` + `templ.WithFragments("name")`: the handler renders the whole page and only the named fragment's output is sent (templ.guide/syntax-and-usage/fragments).
- **htmx essay "Template Fragments"** (htmx.org/essays/template-fragments): the same idea across Django render_block, jinja2-fragments, Chill and others. The htmx-native client half of it is `hx-select`.
- fluent-html has neither: the 422 path in the template is two view functions and a request-header branch.

## Problem

`.submit(route, { invalid: ids.form })` routes a 422 to `#form` with `swap:outerMorph` (swap-verbs.ts:202). Because the status bag carries no `select:`, the 422 body must be the form region alone, or the whole page nests inside the form. So every validated form ships this, verbatim from the template (sign-in.controller.ts:26):

```ts
reply.code(422).renderView(isHtmxRequest(request) ? LoginForm(props) : LoginPage(props));
```

That costs three things per form: an exported second entry point for the form region, a branch on a request header, and a drop from the contracted `renderPage`/`renderFragment` to the unchecked `renderView`. The template spends 9 comment lines (sign-in.controller.ts:16-24) and answer.ts:32-34 explaining why the escape hatch is the intended path.

## Evidence (executed)

1. **Browser, real bundles** (`node $E9/probe/select-probe.mjs`; Chromium; the lib's pinned 4.0.0-beta6 and the template-served 4.0.0). Page: header, `#main-content` (card chrome + `#login-form`), footer. Form built with fluent's `hx()` and a 422 status bag. Server answers 422 twice.

   | 422 body | select | headers | #main-content | forms | 2nd submit forms | bytes |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | A full document (today's bag) | no | 3 after 2 submits (2 after 1) | 3 | 1 | 1 (nested) | 466 |
   | B full document | `#login-form` | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 485 |
   | D Layout htmx arm `[Title, #main-content]` | `#login-form` | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 375 |
   | F form region alone (today's handlers) | `#login-form` | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 296 |
   | C form region alone (today) | no | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 277 |
   | E page without `#login-form` | `#login-form` | 1 | 1 | **0** | n/a | 98 |

   Identical on both bundles. D is what the template's `renderPage` actually sends on an htmx request (layout.view.ts:303-305), and the title updates too. F proves the change is additive: unmodified dual-path handlers keep working.
2. **Emission** (`$E9/tpl`, patched template, `npx tsx emitprobe.ts`): `Form().nav(signInRoutes.signin(), { invalid: authIds.loginForm })` renders `hx-status:422="swap:outerMorph target:#login-form select:#login-form push:false"`, the exact attribute used in rows B/D/F.
3. **Types** (`$E9/tpl`): with the one-key verb change and the sign-in handler rewritten to `return reply.code(422).renderPage(LoginPage(props))` (import of `isHtmxRequest` and `LoginForm` dropped), `npx tsc --noEmit` reports 88 errors before and 88 after, diff empty. The 88 are the unprocessed `[module:*]` markers of the raw template.
4. **Fleet reach** (`node $E9/dual.mjs`, ternary `isHtmxRequest(...) ? Comp(...) : Page(...)`):
   - 42 sites in 21 repos; canonical era 36 in 15 of 16 repos.
   - 13 of the 36 are the template sign-in copied into each app; 23 are app-authored forms (gzs/stem-50 x9, na-cent x6, fl-um x2, website-sales-funnel x2, sportoawards x2, everyframe-composer x1, stojnica x1).
   - In 33/36 the page component's defining file calls the form component, so the page already contains the region `select:` picks.
   - Plus 10 `if (isHtmxRequest(request)) return reply.renderView(...)` sites in 3 canonical repos, and 50 app-authored `code(422).renderView` sites in 14 canonical repos (403 fleet, 43 repos).
5. **Agents**: 4/4 phase-2 runs (blind1, blind2, guided1, guided2) reproduced the branch plus a 2-4 line comment justifying `renderView` (feature.patch lines cited above).
6. **Byte cost of the client-side pick** (popri sign-in, `npx tsx $E9/bytes-popri.mts`): form region 2,278 B vs Layout htmx arm 6,688 B (+4,410 B per rejected submit). The server-side templ variant was prototyped too: walking the built page for `_id === "login-form"` returns a subtree whose render is byte-identical to `LoginForm(props)` (2,278 B), at 41.8 µs vs 21.5 µs for the form alone and 46.3 µs for the whole arm (2,000 iterations each).

## Why it matters

- impact 2: every validated form drops a branch, a second exported entry point and the unchecked render path.
- pain 2: without the branch the page nests inside the form; with it, the two shapes stay in sync by discipline alone (F-B-107 / C-54 measured that an unrooted 422 view deletes the target).
- reach 3: 15 of 16 canonical repos, and every generated feature with a form (4/4 agents).
- Dimensions: invariant-safety (renderPage is contracted, renderView is not), decision-closure (one shape), context-economy (htmx.md:302-312, fastify.md:205 and half of CLAUDE.md:274 go away).
- Converge: the page that rendered the form on GET is the same function that answers the 422, so the `#form` id is present by construction. The residual failure (row E) needs a page that lacks the id it was submitted from.

## Rough idea (not a design)

Template-only, lane `template`: add `select: options.invalid.selector` in `withInvalidTarget`; rewrite the sign-in handler to `return reply.code(422).renderPage(LoginPage(props))`; delete sign-in.controller.ts:16-24, answer.ts:32-34, and the guideline idiom. No codemod is required (row F). If the 2.9x 422 payload ever matters, the templ-style `fragmentOf(view, id)` is the lib follow-up (it needs the protected `_id` and the `Rooted<N>` brand); it is not needed to close the branch.
