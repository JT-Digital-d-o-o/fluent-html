# The .cssProp() property union is frozen at TypeScript 5.9 lib.dom

## Problem
`.cssProp(name, value)` is the sanctioned hatch for a CSS property with no Tailwind utility. It emits `[name:value]`, which is extractor-visible and works under variants.

Its `name` union is generated from TypeScript 5.9.3's `CSSStyleDeclaration`, which lags the platform. Three canonical repos hit the wall and left a comment saying so:

```ts
// sportoawards entry.components.ts:174
const growingTextarea: Styler = (t) => t.setStyle("field-sizing: content").resize("y");
// everyframe home.view.ts:295
.setStyles({ animationTimeline: "view()", animationRange: "entry 5% entry 45%" })
```

The fallbacks are runtime inline styles. They cannot take a variant (`md:` or `motion-safe:`), and `setStyle` replaces any other inline style on the element. everyframe-composer dropped `field-sizing` entirely and counts textarea rows on the server.

## Evidence (executed)
- **Chromium diff** (Chromium 149.0.7827.55 via playwright-core): `Array.from(getComputedStyle(document.documentElement))` gives 436 longhands. **80 of them are not in the union.** Among them:
  - `field-sizing`, `animation-timeline`, `animation-range-start`/`animation-range-end`
  - `view-timeline-name`/`view-timeline-axis`/`view-timeline-inset`, `timeline-scope`, `scroll-timeline-name`
  - `interpolate-size`, `scroll-marker-group`, `scroll-initial-target`, `reading-flow`, `text-autospace`, `overlay`
  - Shorthands such as `animation-range` and `view-timeline` are missing too.
- **Tailwind 4.3.3:** `candidatesToCss` emits all of `[field-sizing:content]`, `[animation-timeline:view()]`, `[animation-range:entry_5%_entry_45%]`, `[view-timeline-name:--sec-a]`, `[timeline-scope:--sec-a,--sec-b]` and `md:[field-sizing:content]`.
- **tsc today:** `.cssProp("field-sizing", …)` and `.cssProp("animation-timeline", …)` both fail with TS2345 "not assignable to parameter of type 'CssPropertyName'".
- **Fleet census** (canonical, `src/core` excluded): these properties appear in 5 repos.
  - animation-timeline: everyframe, fluent-html-home-page, home-page, sportoawards
  - field-sizing: everyframe-composer, sportoawards
  - view-timeline / timeline-scope: sportoawards

## Why it matters
- **Score:** impact 2 × pain 2 × reach 2 / S = 8.
- **The hatch rejects valid CSS.** The escape route it pushes apps onto (`setStyle`) loses variants. On a textarea that also needs `.resize("y")`, it is one call away from clobbering another inline style.
- **No drift signal.** Every TypeScript minor that adds properties widens the union with no signal; nothing tracks it.

## Rough idea (not a design)
- In `gen:vocab`, union lib.dom with a pinned spec property index. The index is a devDependency at generation time, so the zero runtime deps rule holds.
- Keep the union closed and the `--${string}` arm.
- `gen:vocab --check` stays the CI gate (§5.9). The extractor already handles `cssProp` literals.
- Optional follow-up: a Chromium-diff ratchet in the platform watch (C-51).
