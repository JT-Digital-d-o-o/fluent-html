# Template CSP options cannot add media-src, frame-src or form-action, so 6 apps edit core security-headers.ts

## Problem

`SecurityHeadersOptions` lets an app widen only `script-src`, `connect-src` and `img-src`. `media-src` and `frame-src` are absent, so they fall back to `default-src 'self'`, and `form-action` is fixed to `'self'`. An app that serves video from an object store, embeds a player, or posts an upload form straight to storage has two choices: fork core, or ship a feature that the browser silently blocks. The browser reports nothing beyond a console CSP line. Composer's fork records the failure: "every `<video>` fails with a bare 'no supported sources' and nothing in the network tab to explain it".

## Evidence (executed)

- **Per-file divergence** (each app file hashed against every historical template blob of the same path, setup-generated variants separated): `security-headers.ts` is genuinely modified in 8 of 15 apps.
  - 6 add a directive option: mediaSrc in everyframe, everyframe-composer, fl-um and workshop-toni; frameSrc in competify and sportoawards; formAction in fl-um. competify also adds `openerAllowed`.
  - stem-50 and wsfas change comments only.
  - home-page builds its own `mediaSrc` in server.ts:196.
  - Total: 7 of 15 apps need media-src (5), frame-src (2) or form-action (1).
- **Runtime probe** (`csp/probe.mjs`, the template module compiled with esbuild):
  - `helmetOptions({ isProd: true, imgSrc: [...] })` yields `media-src: (absent -> default-src ["'self'"])`, `frame-src: (absent -> default-src ["'self'"])` and `form-action: ["'self'"]`.
- **Type probe** (`csp/opts.ts`): `{ mediaSrc: [...] }` and `{ frameSrc: [...] }` are both rejected by `SecurityHeadersOptions` (excess property, `@ts-expect-error` holds). The only way in is to edit core.

## Why it matters

impact 2 x pain 2 x reach 3 / S = 12.

- The failure is silent in production: CSP blocks the media request, and the page renders an empty player.
- Nearly half the canonical fleet re-derived the same three options.
- The update path does not resync src/ (template decision 2026-08-14), so each new scaffold repeats the discovery.

Dimensions: silent-failure, evolvability.

## Rough idea (not a design)

Add `mediaSrc`, `frameSrc` and `formAction` to the options type and to the directives object, each defaulting to `["'self'"]`. This leaves today's policy byte-identical for apps that pass nothing. The existing helmetOptions unit test gains three rows. The type is the enforcement layer, and no prose is added.
