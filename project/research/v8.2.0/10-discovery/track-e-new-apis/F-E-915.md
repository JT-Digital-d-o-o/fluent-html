# Value unions that match what the families already render

($EG = <scratch>/track-e/E-gaps)

## Problem

Four families render every Tailwind 4.3.3 value through their prefix emitters but type a narrower set:

- `minH`/`minW` take no spacing step (template.txt:57, :63), while `w`, `h` and `maxH` embed `TailwindSpacing`. The brand's 44px tap target becomes `.minH("px", 44)` (10 sites), `.minH("px", 160)` 12 sites, `.minH("px", 48)` 8 sites.
- `aspect` takes `auto | square | video` (vocab.ts:163); `aspect-9/16` and `aspect-[9/16]` are valid Tailwind, so apps write `cssProp("aspect-ratio", "9/16")` (7 literal sites in 2 repos).
- `grow` takes `0`; `flex` takes 4 shorthand keywords: `grow-[30]`, `flex-[7]` have no spelling.
- `TailwindColor` has `[${string}]` but not `[${string}]/${number}`: `bg-[#0B1520]/45` fails.

## Evidence (executed)

1. **Design corpus compile** (`$EG/design`: every eslint-plugin 4.1.0 autofix chain for the 4,301 mapped tokens of 249 canonical design files, emitted into one TS file, theme tokens augmented so only real gaps remain): 274 distinct tokens / 7,724 occurrences fail tsc on 8.1.0. On the prototype, 118 distinct / 835 occurrences compile: outline 282 (filed as F-E-914) plus this finding's 553 (min-h/min-w 182 in 6 repos, aspect 155 in 5, grow/flex arbitrary 108 in 3, `[hex]/opacity` 108 in 4).
2. **TS census**: 106 arbitrary `.minH`/`.minW` calls in 16/16 canonical repos; 105 are multiples of 4px and 57 land exactly on the `BaseSpacing` list. 7 literal aspect-ratio hatches in everyframe and everyframe-composer; the runtime-computed ratios (`setStyles({ aspectRatio: ... })`) stay as they are.
3. **Prototype** (`$EG/lib`): the unions above, the aspect row as a group (keyword | ratio), `+arb` on the flex shorthand union, an arbitrary arm on grow's variant key. `gen:vocab --check` OK; vocab-validity, class-vocab and gen-types 1007/1007 with oracle samples for the new values; `$EG/vocab-probe` renders `min-h-11 min-w-11`, `aspect-9/16 aspect-[16/8.4]`, `grow-[30] flex-[7]`, `bg-[#0B1520]/45`, all valid in the 4.3.3 oracle.

## Why it matters

impact 2 (the eslint autofix and the pure prior both produce these spellings and get a tsc error with no successor), pain 1 (unit overloads and cssProp work, at the cost of an off-scale value and an untyped string), reach 3 (16 repos). Hurts prior-alignment and context-economy.

## Rough idea (not a design)

Type-only widening in the template unions and two vocab rows; emitters unchanged; extractor and eslint re-derive (guardrail 9). Not included: Tailwind 4's open numeric spacing (`w-4.5`, `w-70`, 57 design occurrences in 5 repos), which reopens the closed-union decision and goes to overflow as decision-gated.
