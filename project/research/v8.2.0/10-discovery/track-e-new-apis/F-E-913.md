# Swapped feedback announces itself

($EG = <scratch>/track-e/E-gaps)

## Peer capability

- **Phoenix** generated `core_components`: the `flash` component renders `role="alert"`, so every LiveView flash is announced without the app doing anything.
- **GOV.UK Design System**: the error summary that appears after a failed submit takes focus and is announced; field errors are linked to their inputs.
- **fluent-html + template**: the lib types `setRole("alert" | "status")` and `setAria({ live })`; the template's Alert and error paragraphs use neither, and in an htmx app every error and confirmation arrives by swap, so a screen-reader user hears nothing after pressing Submit.

## Problem

The template's Alert is `Div(P(message)).apply(noticeBox(type))...` (shared/ui/form.ts:87-96): tone colors, no role. Its sign-in form renders the credential failure as `IfThen(error, (msg) => P(msg))` (login.view.ts:39) inside the region the 422 swaps in; 6 sibling template sites do the same. 15 canonical repos carry a copy of `shared/ui/form.ts` with Alert; 14 have no role. The status-shaped fragment targets apps build (score summaries, previews, status cards, a toast region) are plain divs: 1 of 13 is a live region.

## Evidence (executed)

1. **Runtime** (`node $EG/live/ax.mjs`): Chromium via Playwright, htmx 4.0.0 (home-page/public/js/htmx.min.js), a form posting to a route answered with 422 and the form plus the template Alert, swapped by `outerMorph`; after the swap, `Accessibility.getFullAXTree`:
   - template Alert as shipped: no node with role alert or status, `live` none.
   - with `.setRole("alert")`: role alert, `live` assertive.
   - with `.setRole("status")`: role status, `live` polite.
2. **Census** (16 canonical repos, app code):
   - Alert defined in 15 `shared/ui/form.ts` copies, role or live in 1 (everyframe-composer form.ts:138).
   - 140 `Alert({` call sites in 16 repos.
   - 159 conditional error or notice renders (`IfThen(error | notice | message | flash ...)`) in 16 repos; 2 carry a role or live on the line; 44 render a bare `P`.
   - Swap targets (`node $EG/live/live.mjs`, `.fragment/.search/.onChange/Partial` first argument, `#main-content` excluded): 94 ids in 10 repos; 13 status-shaped by name (score, summary, preview, status, toast, progress) in 7 repos, 1 live region (competify judgeIds.scoreSummary).
3. **Placement trap**: stem-50's toast region is "replaced wholesale by a response partial" (core/layout/toast.ts:48-59). A live region must stay in the DOM to announce a change; a role on a node an outerHTML swap replaces is a new region each time. `.fragment` morphs (outerMorph keeps the node), `.poll` swaps outerHTML, so the role belongs on the kept element, or on an inner element for polled targets.

## Why it matters

impact 2 (error and confirmation messages are the whole feedback loop of a form; without a role a screen-reader user cannot tell a rejected sign-in from a page that did nothing), pain 2, reach 3 (16 repos). axe cannot see this class: it inspects static DOM, and a role-less paragraph is valid markup. Hurts silent-failure.

## Rough idea (not a design)

Template-layer change: Alert sets its role by tone, the template's error paragraphs render Alert, and the template's status-shaped components set role=status on the element the swap keeps. Enforcement is the component default (every copy inherits it at the next template pull); a lint cannot tell a status message from body text. No prose to delete.
