# defineTheme has no animation family, so 8 apps hand-write 32 @keyframes and stem-50 dismisses toasts with a server round trip

## Problem

The rules say tokens come from `defineTheme()` and nothing is hand-written in CSS (`@theme` blocks included). But `defineTheme` covers only colors, spacing, fontSize, radius, shadow and fonts. Tailwind v4's `--animate-*` and `--ease-*` namespaces and `@keyframes` (which v4 accepts inside `@theme`) have no typed path. `.animate()` is closed to the five stock names plus an arbitrary bracket.

Apps that need motion therefore take one of two routes:

- Hand-write CSS in `public/css/styles.css` and reach it through `.animate("[name_620ms_cubic-bezier(...)_both]")`, `.cssClass(...)` or raw class rules.
- Avoid motion. stem-50's toast cannot fade itself out, so it fires a `load delay:4s` request to a dedicated endpoint that answers an empty region. The file's header gives the reason: keyframes need hand-written CSS.

## Evidence (executed)

- **CSS census** (each canonical app's `public/css/styles.css`, comments stripped): 32 `@keyframes` in 8 of 15 apps.
  - Per app: home-page 11, everyframe 7, wsfas 5, workshop-toni 4, fluent-html-home-page 2, competify 1, composer 1, sportoawards 1.
  - Hand-written `@theme` animation vars: 9 `--animate-*` (everyframe 3, wsfas 6) and 1 `--ease-*` (wsfas).
- **TS census:** 10 arbitrary `.animate("[...]")` sites (everyframe 8, wsfas 1, home-page 1) and 6 arbitrary `.ease("[...]")` sites (na-cent 5, wsfas 1).
- **Tailwind oracle** (`tw-animate.mjs` and `theme-animate-e2e.mjs`, tailwindcss 4.3.3 compile): a `@theme` block with `--animate-toast-out`, `--ease-brand` and nested `@keyframes` emits `.animate-toast-out { animation: var(--animate-toast-out); }`, `.ease-brand`, both `@keyframes`, and `motion-reduce:animate-none` (6/6 present). The tokens are lifted from stem-50's toast, workshop-toni's idle loop and wsfas's `--ease-brand`.
- **Type prototype** (scratch copy of the lib):
  - `ThemeSpec` gains `animate`, `ease` and `keyframes`.
  - `FluentCustomAnimate` and `FluentCustomEase` seams join the two unions.
  - The app's `declare module` derives keys with `ThemeKeys<typeof tokens, "animate">`.
  - `Div().animate("toast-out").ease("brand")` compiles, and `.animate("toast-oot")` is rejected (`@ts-expect-error` holds).
  - `node --test` on define-theme, forms, form-for and fluent-styling-v2: 348/348 pass.

## Why it matters

impact 2 x pain 2 x reach 3 / M = 6.

- More than half the canonical fleet runs a second styling path that the rules forbid and that the type system, extractor and lint cannot see.
- One app built a request-per-toast mechanism to stay inside the rules.
- The family is a Tailwind v4 `@theme` namespace, so this adds to the one `defineTheme` rather than adding a theming API.

Dimensions: decision-closure, context-economy, prior-alignment.

## Rough idea (not a design)

Add `animate`, `ease` and `keyframes` to `ThemeSpec`, and open `TailwindAnimate`/`TailwindEase` through two seams. This lands as vocab-generator rows (guardrail 9), not hand edits. The extractor's `themeToCss` gains the two namespaces plus `@keyframes` emission. The type layer holds the token names. A lint or ci check can later flag `@keyframes` in an app's styles.css, and the stem-50 dismiss endpoint becomes `.animate("toast-out")`.
