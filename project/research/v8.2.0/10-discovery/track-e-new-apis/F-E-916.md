# Three roots the designs use and the vocabulary lacks

($EG = <scratch>/track-e/E-gaps)

## Problem

The fleet's design files are the documented input agents translate into fluent-html. Three roots they use have no method:

- `place-items-center` (369 occurrences, 5 repos): pruned in 8.0.0 because TS had 0 call sites (CHANGELOG.md:119). The TS side has 0 because the designs get rewritten: popri says so in code ("Centring is flex rather than the design's `place-items-center`: identical for one child, and fluent has no `placeItems` method", stylers.ts:387-388). The stand-in `items("center").justify("center")` sets justify-content, not justify-items, so it matches only for a single child.
- `justify-self-*` (79, 3 repos) and `justify-items-center` (7, 1 repo): `self` is align-self; there is no inline-axis counterpart, and eslint-plugin 4.1.0 maps both to `.justify("self-end")` / `.justify("items-center")`, which tsc rejects.
- `normal-case` (15, 4 repos): `uppercase`/`lowercase`/`capitalize` exist, nothing resets them at a breakpoint.

## Evidence (executed)

1. Design census over 249 Tailwind design files in 15 canonical repos (`$EG/design/collect.cjs`): the counts above, 470 occurrences in 7 repos (gzs/stem-50, home-page, popri, workshop-toni, projects-template, everyframe, everyframe-composer).
2. TS census: 0 cssProp `place-items`/`justify-self` sites, 1 documented workaround (popri), 69 stand-in centering chains in 12 repos (critic's gap-size.mjs, rerun).
3. Prototype (`$EG/lib`): three rows plus their template unions; `gen:vocab --check` OK; vocab tests 1007/1007; renders `grid place-items-center justify-self-end` and `uppercase md:normal-case`, valid in the 4.3.3 oracle; the coverage watch drops the `normal-case` backlog row.

## Why it matters

impact 1, pain 1, reach 2: the gap costs a near-equivalent rewrite (place-items), an untyped cssProp (justify-self, normal-case) or a failing autofix. Hurts prior-alignment for design translation.

## Rough idea (not a design)

Three vocab rows. Converge check for curation: `justifySelf` and `normalCase` have no second spelling today; `placeItems` competes with the items + justify stand-in, which is not equivalent for multi-child grids, so the re-add answers L-222's "conscious re-add with census evidence" with design-corpus census rather than TS call sites.
