## Conventions

- **Paths** are relative to the org root (`fluent-html/...`, `projects-template/...`). `:n` is a line at the HEADs in the re-anchoring notes.
- **Release keys** (lockstep.md §2 with the Track E rows): `fluent-html 8.1.1` (step 1), `plugin 4.2.0` (2), `extractor main` (3), `G1` (4), `fluent-html 4b` (4b, repo commit, no release), `template 3.8.0` (5), `fluent-html 8.2.0` (6), `plugin 4.3.0` (6b), `G2` (7), `fluent-html 7b` (7b, repo commit, no release), `template 3.9.0` (8), `fluent-html 9.0.0` (9), `G3` (10). Two keys lockstep names without a step number: `template 9.0.0 pass` (§3.5, no code) and `plugin re-sweep after 9.0.0` (K4).
- **Artifacts.** `pm-task` on every row. `changelog`: an entry in `60-rollout/staged/<repo>/<release-dir>/CHANGELOG.patch`. `readme`: a hunk in `60-rollout/staged/<repo>/<release-dir>/README.patch`; the documented surface is `README.md` plus `REFERENCE.md` (fluent-html), `README.md` (plugin, extractor), and `README.md` plus `templates/web/README.md` and `templates/web/CLAUDE.md` (projects-template, RFC-A-02's "Template docs"). Release dirs: `fluent-html/{8.1.1,4b,8.2.0,9.0.0}`, `fluent-html-eslint-plugin/{4.2.0,4.3.0}`, `fluent-html-tailwind-extractor/main`, `projects-template/{3.8.0,3.9.0}`. The PM task that ships a step applies that step's patches.
- **Depends on.** `<item> @ <release>` names another row. `release: <key>` waits for that step to publish (a guidelines wave: to land on guidelines main). `decision:` is a human call (the story carries `<!-- hill: uphill -->`). `measure:` is a measurement the step waits for.
- **PM ownership.** fluent-html's scope: every fluent-html row, the extractor row (the extractor has no `project/pm/`; the task cross-references the extractor repo) and the three guidelines rows. The plugin's scope: the plugin rows. The template's scope: the template rows. One story per release step.
- **Hunk text** for `G1` to `G3`, `fluent-html 4b` and `fluent-html 7b` is in `40-synthesis/guidelines-update.md`; those rows are pm-task only, except RFC-C-01's lib README hunk at 4b.
